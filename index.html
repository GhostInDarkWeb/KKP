📜 Hamara Sankalp

Ghoshna Patra 2026

KKP ka har ek waada aapki zindagi ko behtar banane ke liye. Yeh hain hamare 7 mukhya sankalp:

SANKALP 01

🔧 Har Yuva Ko Kaam

5 saal mein 2 crore naukriyan. Har district mein skill development centre. Free career counseling.

SANKALP 02

📖 Mufat Shiksha

School to college — 100% free education. Scholarship har mehanti student ke liye. Digital libraries.

SANKALP 03

🏘️ Garibi Se Azaadi

Har gariib parivar ko pucca ghar, bijli, paani aur ration. Garibi rekha 50% neeche laana.

SANKALP 04

⚖️ Saman Adhikar

Koi bhedbhav nahi. Equal pay, equal rights. Women safety aur reservation mein sudhar.

SANKALP 05

🌱 Yuva Neta

Yuva ko politics mein mauke. 25 saal ke candidate bhi khade ho sakte hain. New leadership.

SANKALP 06

🏥 Muft Swasthya

Har gaon mein clinic, har shehar mein hospital. Free treatment aur dawai sabke liye.

SANKALP 07

📡 Digital India 2.0

Har gaon mein internet. Free WiFi. Online jobs aur digital services har nagrik tak.
